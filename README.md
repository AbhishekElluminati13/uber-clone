## All Ride Types Supported

A strong ride-hailing platform does not force you into a single service category. Our Uber clone supports ten distinct ride types, each addressing a specific market segment and revenue opportunity:

**Economy Ride** serves standard point-to-point trips at base fare rates, automatically matched to the nearest available driver. This is the everyday workhorse of your platform and the category that builds your initial rider base.

**Premium Ride** operates in selected vehicle categories for corporate travelers, airport transfers, and clients who prioritize comfort over price. Premium rides carry a higher average fare value and generate better unit economics per trip.

**Carpool Ride** matches multiple riders traveling in the same direction in a single vehicle, lowering the cost per rider while increasing revenue per trip for drivers and improving vehicle utilization during off-peak hours.

**Bike Taxi** provides faster, more affordable rides on two-wheelers for shorter distances. In high-traffic urban areas, bike taxis solve the last-mile problem that four-wheel vehicles cannot address efficiently.

**Auto (Tuk-Tuk) Ride** supports three-wheeler auto-rickshaw bookings — the dominant shared transport mode across South Asia, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Latin America. For operators entering these markets, auto rides are not a niche; they are a primary category.

**XL Ride** accommodates groups of five or more passengers in larger vehicles — SUVs, vans, or minivans — in a single booking. Group travel, family trips, and luggage-heavy airport transfers increase per-trip revenue without adding operational complexity.

**Rentals** offer hourly or half-day vehicle bookings with a driver for business travelers, tourists, and corporate clients who need flexible transport. The revenue per engagement is higher than standard point-to-point rides.

**Intercity Ride** handles prebooked trips between cities or towns at fixed pricing for the full route. This is a high-ticket category that operates independently of your daily city-level booking volume.

**EV Ride** allows riders to specifically request an electric vehicle, enabling operators to register EV drivers separately, carry lower running costs per kilometer, and serve sustainability-focused markets.

**Corporate Ride** connects pre-scheduled trips to corporate accounts with centralized billing, employee-level booking controls, and monthly invoicing — turning one-time riders into consistent, contracted business relationships.

## All Modules: Apps and Web Panels

Every role in a ride-hailing operation has different responsibilities, different data needs, and different decisions to make daily. A platform that forces everyone through a single interface creates bottlenecks and blind spots. Our Uber clone includes purpose-built apps and panels for every stakeholder in your operation.

### Rider App

The primary interface your customers use to book, track, and pay for every ride. Key capabilities include multi-channel booking (app, phone, SMS, WhatsApp), instant and scheduled ride booking, real-time driver tracking, fare estimation before confirmation, multiple payment options, ratings and reviews, emergency and safety features, ride preferences, and complete ride history with receipts. A companion user web panel allows customers to manage bookings from a browser when they prefer not to use the mobile app.

### Driver App

The operational tool every driver uses to receive trips, navigate routes, and manage earnings. The app handles driver registration and document verification, trip request management, in-app navigation and route guidance, an earnings dashboard, an in-app wallet, ride history, online and offline availability toggle, a driver ratings system, direct communication with riders, and SOS emergency access.

### Admin Web Panel

The operational control center for day-to-day business management. Through the admin panel, you manage all rider and driver accounts, monitor rides in real time, configure fares and pricing structures, manage payments and transactions, set commission rules, track revenue through analytics dashboards, manage zones and geofences, handle disputes and support requests, run promotions and discounts, and generate detailed operational reports.

### Dispatcher Web Panel

Built for manual ride assignment across multiple booking channels — phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and walk-ins. The dispatcher panel provides live map visibility of the full fleet, trip monitoring with intervention capability, management of special ride requirements, separate ride-type lists, cancellation handling, and an in-app wallet. This panel is particularly valuable for businesses that serve customers who do not use smartphone apps and for managing complex bookings during peak hours.

This keeps your operation organized as you scale through partnerships rather than direct ownership of every vehicle.

The super admin manages all subsidiary admin accounts, controls global system settings, accesses consolidated revenue reporting, oversees the complete driver and rider database, manages rewards and incentive programs, reviews audit logs and activity tracking, and runs cross-region analytics. Corporate accounts pay monthly, generate consistent high-volume rides, and reduce your per-ride acquisition cost significantly.

As EV adoption accelerates, this panel positions your operation ahead of where the industry is heading.

## Revenue Streams Built Into the Platform

A ride-hailing business generates revenue from multiple sources simultaneously. The platform is configured to capture all of them:

**Ride Commission** is the core revenue model — you set your commission percentage per vehicle category, service type, or region, and earn a percentage of every fare completed on your platform.

**Driver Subscription Fees** give high-frequency drivers the option of a fixed weekly or monthly charge in exchange for reduced commissions, providing you with predictable recurring revenue independent of trip volume.

**Surge Margin and Priority Zones** automatically increase fares when demand exceeds driver availability — during rush hours, bad weather, major events, or public holidays — with zone-specific rules that activate based on location and time.

**Cancellation Fees** charge riders who cancel after a defined window, protecting driver earnings and platform revenue. The cancellation window, fee amount, and driver split are all configurable per service type.

**Booking and Service Fees** apply a fixed or percentage-based charge per ride at checkout, covering platform operating costs and adding a revenue layer that compounds as your booking volume grows.

**Corporate Ride Contracts** generate consistent monthly income from businesses that pre-approve credit, operate on centralized billing, and produce high ride volumes without the acquisition cost of individual riders.

**In-App Advertising** turns your growing rider base into an advertising inventory — local businesses, restaurants, retail brands, and event promotions pay to place content in front of your users during booking or post-trip screens.

**Driver Registration Fees** charge new drivers a one-time fee upon joining after document verification, creating a revenue stream that scales directly with your driver acquisition efforts.

**Fleet Partnerships** generate commission revenue on every trip completed by partner fleet vehicles, along with partnership fees that add a recurring income layer on top of standard trip commissions.
